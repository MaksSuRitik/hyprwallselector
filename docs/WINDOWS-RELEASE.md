Windows 10/11 x64 installer. Download the **Setup.exe** asset; dependencies are included.

- Image and video wallpaper carousel; Alt+T and tray controls.
- Starts at sign-in by default. Disable this in Settings if preferred.
- Video pauses on fullscreen apps, battery, lock and display-off.
- Direct D3D11 video playback with a bounded frame queue and codec-sized GPU reference pool.
- Faster video switching and carousel previews, without transcoding the source files.
- Reuses the video surface between video wallpapers and releases decoders when switching to images.
- Optional high CPU priority checkbox in Settings, applied now and at startup.
- Update notifications from this repository; installation preserves preferences.

The installer is currently unsigned. Wallpapers and preferences are preserved when uninstalling.
Source and third-party notices are included with the installation.
